# Link Saver — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

Link Saver is a browser extension that lets you save and organize links on your New Tab page. This policy explains what data it handles.

## What Link Saver stores

- The links you save (title, address, site icon address, notes, tags), and your workspaces, categories and settings.
- All of this is stored **only on your computer**, in your browser's local extension storage.

## Open tabs

To show the "Open tabs" panel, Link Saver reads the title, address and icon of your open tabs. This information is shown to you and is saved only when you choose to save a tab. It is never sent anywhere.

## Backups

If you turn on automatic backup, Link Saver writes a JSON file with your saved links to a folder **you choose** on your computer (or to a folder inside Downloads). If that folder is synced by a cloud service such as Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, that service's own privacy policy applies to the copy it syncs.

## Site icons

To show icons for your saved links, Link Saver may request the icon from Google's public favicon service (`www.google.com/s2/favicons`), which sends the **website's domain name** (for example `github.com`) to Google. No other information is sent. You can turn this off in Settings → General → "Site icons from Google"; Link Saver then uses only the icons Chrome already has.

## What Link Saver does not do

- No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
- Your data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
- Link Saver does not read the content of the pages you visit.
